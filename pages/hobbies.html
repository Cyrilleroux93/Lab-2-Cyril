My Hobbies

I have had most of my hobbies for quite a long time. The amount of time I spend on each one changes, but cooking, travelling and gaming are probably the three that have stayed with me the longest.

Cooking

Cooking is probably one of the hobbies I spend the most time on. I like trying new recipes but I also like changing things and experimenting depending on what I have at home.

I also like trying new kitchen tools. This often means buying something, using it once and then forgetting about it for several years before suddenly finding a reason to use it again.

The picture here is the main dish I cooked for New Year. It was poached cod with buttered shrimp and lemon sauce, served with a simple mash and broccolini.

Travelling

I really enjoy travelling and discovering new places. A lot of my travelling is connected to work, but I also travel for myself whenever I get the chance.

A big part of travelling for me is food. I like trying local dishes and I am generally not very scared of ordering something I have never tried before. I think food is one of the easiest ways to discover a bit more about a place.

The picture here is one I took of the Brooklyn Bridge during a trip to New York.

Gaming

I have been playing games for a long time, but the type of games I enjoy has changed quite a lot over the years.

When I was younger I used to spend a lot of time playing large online games and being part of gaming communities. These days I prefer games with a world to explore and a story to discover.

I generally try to stay away from online games now because I often find the communities around them too toxic, so I prefer games I can enjoy at my own pace.
