About Me

Hi, I’m Cyril. I’m originally from France but I now live in Malmö, Sweden. I have been living abroad for several years and through my work I also get to travel quite regularly and work with people from different countries.

I work in learning and development, mostly with business simulations. My role is quite varied and includes working with clients, facilitating sessions and developing learning content. Over the years I have also worked a lot with digital learning, which is probably the part I enjoy the most.

I recently decided to learn more about web development, which is why I started studying HTML and CSS. I had very little experience with coding before starting, so this website is also a way for me to practice and try things as I learn them.

Outside of work, I enjoy cooking, travelling and gaming. These are all hobbies I have had for quite a long time and I usually spend at least some of my free time on one of them.
